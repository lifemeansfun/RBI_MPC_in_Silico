## Establish the evidence contract

First inspect the implementation, configuration, protocols, existing evidence and ongoing work. Reconcile completed, partial, failed and unverified work. Identify the central research question and claims, the closest prior work, and the highest-impact scientific blockers. Before expanding the architecture, determine whether available data and affordable experiments can answer the question.

Create a concise, fixed evidence matrix. For each mandatory criterion record the claim, required evidence, reproducible check, justified acceptance condition and current **PASS / FAIL / UNVERIFIED** status:

1. **Contribution:** A precise, important question and a defensible contribution compared with the closest primary literature. Architectural complexity and additional agents do not establish novelty.
2. **Data and timing:** Source provenance, release/vintage availability, information cutoffs, missingness, label validity and leakage controls. Separate decision dates from publication and availability dates. Address hindsight in personas and possible historical knowledge in pretrained models.
3. **Identification and controls:** Fair simple and strong baselines and controls that distinguish the proposed mechanism from extra information, persona content and additional calls or compute. Cover informative policy outcomes and regimes. State which comparisons match information and resources, and justify differences.
4. **Actual empirical evidence:** Execute the experiments required by the claims. A homogeneous all-hold panel cannot establish useful rate prediction through accuracy alone. Mock runs, synthetic fixtures, cached replay and unit tests cannot replace independent live-model evidence where that evidence is necessary. A well-supported null or negative finding is acceptable.
5. **Measurement and statistics:** Correct voting rules, aligned joins and separate rate, direction, stance and formal dissent labels. Reservations are not automatically dissent. Justify sample adequacy, meeting-level analysis, temporal dependence, stochastic replication, effect sizes, uncertainty and multiple comparisons. More calls on the same meetings do not create more independent meetings.
6. **Reproducibility:** Reconstruct central results from documented sources, commands, dependencies, model settings, prompts and configurations. Preserve raw outputs, hashes, environment, costs and failures. Verify the integrated final version, not only isolated components.
7. **Claim validity:** Distinguish forecasting, retrospective reconstruction and simulator-local effects. Claims about cognition, equilibrium, welfare or real policy require corresponding evidence. Separate verified facts, repository assertions, assumptions and hypotheses.

Do not lower acceptance conditions to finish. If a scientific correction changes the question or scope, preserve the original claim and explain the change; do not silently mark its unmet criterion as passed or replace it with a trivial contribution.

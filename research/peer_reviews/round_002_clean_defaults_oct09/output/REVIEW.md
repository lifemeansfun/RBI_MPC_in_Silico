# Scientific snapshot peer review

**Independence declaration.** Before inspecting the snapshot, I recorded that inherited instructions and context contained no prior scientific judgment about this project. That record is in [context.txt](context.txt). This is an AI peer review, not independent human review or independent model-family diversity. I used no other agents, parent-project material, Git/history, memory, previous research reviews or credentials. I inspected the scientific scope document explicitly identified by WORKINGS, not separate implementation plans. Source annotation methods were treated as scientific evidence, not as a previous review verdict.

**Overall assessment.** The snapshot contains a substantial, reproducible simulation-engineering foundation and materially useful source curation. It does not establish that an LLM committee predicts monetary policy, that deliberation improves predictions, that generated personas reproduce policymakers' preferences, or that intervention differences identify real-world causal effects. The decisive issue is evidentiary: the complete current study consists of deterministic mock generations on eight already-exposed meetings, all with unchanged committee rates and stance. Its own qualification of that evidence is appropriately cautious. Several older entry points and the report generator still overstate what the system establishes.

**Evidence convention.** “Verified” below means a review computation, code inspection or direct retained-source inspection. “Repository-reported” identifies an assertion that I did not independently establish. “Inference” identifies my interpretation; recommendations do not claim to be observed facts. File hashes establish consistency with the supplied snapshot, not authenticity, contemporaneous publication or a prospective freeze.

I used the exact supplied runtime path, without resolving its symlink. The environment was Python 3.11.16 on macOS arm64; package versions are recorded in [stress_probes.log](stress_probes.log). All new outputs and temporary files are under this directory. The harness disables dotenv discovery, gives executed probes a credential-free environment, blocks socket connections and restricts Python file writes to review outputs, apart from `/dev/null`. The central executable evidence is [probes.py](probes.py), [source_probes.py](source_probes.py), [stress_probes.py](stress_probes.py), [additional_checks.py](additional_checks.py), and [annotation_compare.py](annotation_compare.py). All passed the repository's replay verification, with zero provider calls. Separate scoring code then read the actual final-ballot artifacts without using the repository metric functions. The findings agree with the saved diagnostic summary. See [probes_retry.log](probes_retry.log), [independent_metrics.json](independent_metrics.json), and the `replay_*` output directories.

The mock ignores substantive economic reasoning and peer argument content: it emits zero rate changes and assigns neutral stance to named Goyal/Varma identities in its usual branch. Aliasing the names removes that branch. Thus the masked-versus-unmasked effect is an implementation consequence, not evidence of model memorization, identity-based economic reasoning or successful contamination control. Repeated seeds produce the same mock decisions; zero variation here provides no estimate of live-model uncertainty. These conclusions follow directly from [llm_client.py](../src/infra/llm_client.py), especially `_generate_mock`, and the saved raw generation records.

The current protocol correctly calls this an exposed historical procedural diagnostic, disables provider calls and denies empirical superiority. Those qualifications deserve credit. The historical “live” CSV reports dissent F1 0.8333, while other legacy tables report 25-meeting 76% rate/stance accuracy. The retained old checkpoints do not contain the complete raw generation/request/schema evidence of the current study. Their filenames and aggregate scores cannot authenticate independent live-model evidence, and their labels/data differ from the current source-linked panel. I therefore do not combine those numbers with the current results or treat them as independently reproduced. Evidence: [legacy table](../results/legacy_saved/tables/holdout_validation_live_metrics.csv), [legacy checkpoint](../data/checkpoints/holdout_meeting_2024-06-07.json), [legacy inspection log](legacy_and_literature.log).

This directly shows why substituting the later monthly number changes the information set. The four extracted CPI documents are retained in `cpi_source_*.txt`. I also inspected first/last-panel GDP and Fed source documents: GDP growth -23.9% and 7.8%, and Fed target upper bounds 0.25% and 5.50%, agree with the selected packets. Eight intended macro fields remain absent, including core and food CPI, output gap, industrial production, liquidity, exchange rate, oil and monsoon departure. This is preferable to invented or zero-filled observations, but narrows the scientific question. The full open-economy Taylor rule cannot operate as advertised without a sourced output gap; my probe confirmed its explicit rejection. GDP growth is not a substitute for a measured output gap. The macro prompt also omits each observation's reference period, release date and vintage even though the schema retains them. Agents receive a mixture of monthly CPI, quarterly activity and a policy target without seeing their differing ages. Present those metadata, especially if the intended hypothesis concerns real-time information processing.

The source ledger itself does not exhaustively rule out all intermediate/correction releases, and the documents are currently retained bytes rather than contemporaneously archived downloads. Therefore “available before cutoff” is better supported than “exhaustively complete real-time information set.” The design lacks the complete staff forecasts, expectations and qualitative briefing material that a real committee receives. Input incompleteness, model behavior and institutional effects cannot be cleanly separated in performance differences.

**Dissent is not one interchangeable construct.** The historical records distinguish formal votes, reservations and guidance disagreement. In June 2023 the source says Varma expressed reservations; that need not become an observed vote against the resolution. In August 2021 his personal statement expressly says he votes against the accommodative stance. Keeping those passages separate is defensible. A sensitivity analysis under a broader reservation-inclusive definition would be useful, provided the alternative is specified before scoring rather than chosen for favorable results.

For the later roster, the two retained annotation passes contain 13 decisions and 78 member records. I independently verified 1,048 raw HTML excerpt slices against byte-decoded source files. Committee rate changes and repo levels agree for all 13 decisions; normalized committee stance agrees too. All 78 member rate choices and literal rate-ballot fields agree across passes, including six missing literal ballots in October 2026. These are useful agreement checks, but shared missing values are not 78 observed literal ballots.

There are two formal-stance classification disagreements: Saugata Bhattacharya on 6 August 2025 and Nagesh Kumar on 6 February 2026. The first pass labels neutral; the second leaves formal stance null. The first cites personal statements supporting a status quo vote, while the second more strictly separates personal position from separately documented formal stance voting. The latter Kumar passage explicitly covers both rate and stance, so a broad “explicit vote statement” definition could include it; a narrower “collective resolution vote record” definition could exclude it. This requires a declared construct and adjudication, not an assertion of perfect double-coded agreement. See [annotation_compare_final.log](annotation_compare_final.log), [annotation_comparison.json](annotation_comparison.json), and [disagreements.log](disagreements.log).

The second pass records 77 of 78 reservation fields as unassessed/null, and 43 guidance fields as null, with 35 positive guidance annotations. These must not become negatives for precision/recall or reliability denominators. Matching source excerpts is not itself semantic annotation reliability. Separate-pass provenance is repository-reported; I cannot authenticate human independence or model-family diversity from these files.

The June 2025 edge case is well handled: all six literal rate-table cells say Yes, while Bhattacharya chooses a 25-bps cut and five others choose 50 bps. Exact-magnitude dissent therefore differs from literal No and directional dissent. I confirmed this both in retained records and on the [official RBI minutes page](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=60686). October 2026 remains resolution-only in the supplied evidence. Its calibrated-tightening stance is outside the current three-category simulator schema. Expanded-panel integration needs a versioned stance ontology, dated roster/personas and actual input packets before it can function as an evaluation set. It is already retrospective/exposed, not a replacement untouched holdout. The current October source could not be independently fetched through the web tool; its substantive claims remain retained-source findings.

**Persona construction and construct validity.** I regenerated all seven members' lexical statistics and selected quotations from their dated corpora; every recomputed field matched the frozen manifest. Source hashes and pre-cutoff publication restrictions passed. The current implementation does not insert the legacy hand-set biographies into the dated fit. These are real reproducibility strengths. See [stress_probes.log](stress_probes.log), [extractor.py](../src/persona_engine/extractor.py), and [provenance.py](../src/persona_engine/provenance.py).

The lexical statistic counts words such as inflation, growth and target without sentence-level negation or stance interpretation. Discussing the costs of inflation targeting can increase a “hawk” count just as endorsing tightening can. Current values include positive scores for Goyal and Varma and a slightly negative Bhide score; this is not proof of error, but demonstrates why descriptive word contrast must not be treated as an estimated preference parameter. Quote selection uses the first eligible sentence at a chronological spread, not a validated representation of economic beliefs. No measurement-invariance, convergent-validity or prospective incremental-value result establishes the persona construct.

Useful tests would compare macro-only, anonymous homogeneous, dated persona, shuffled-persona and no-score variants on the same frozen panel. Add independent rubric-based annotation of preference-bearing passages, record disagreements, and assess whether the persona contributes beyond member identity and persistence. Fixed April 2023 personas cannot legitimately backcast earlier meetings as their then-available knowledge; the current research runner correctly rejects that use. Rolling fits are necessary for a longer historical predictive exercise.

**Deliberation and institutional mechanism.** The orchestrator uses four rounds, own-state memory, recipient-specific peer histories and a Governor-last final ballot with visibility of the five other binding ballots. The independent control uses rounds 1 and 4 without peer arguments or Governor ballot visibility. Earlier-rounds removes round 3 from final context but still provides earlier own/peer states and current ballots to the Governor. These are distinct interventions; they do not isolate a single quantity called “persuasion.” The Governor's asymmetric information is a simulator choice rather than verified historical deliberation procedure. Prompt examples also instantiate zero rate change and the previous stance; a live-model anchoring effect is a hypothesis to test, not demonstrated by mock results.

The strict rule path is much clearer than the original scope document: it separates majority, binary equality resolved by the Governor, and synthetic multiaction fragmentation. My exhaustive probe of all 729 six-member profiles over {-25,0,25} accepted the correct 279 majority/binary-tie cases and rejected all 450 fragmented cases. The current study uses rejection. This is good finite-domain engineering evidence, not exhaustive validation of every legal attendance configuration. The retained Act text places majority/casting provisions in §45ZI and publication in §45ZL; public legal PDF retrieval attempts failed, so that cross-check relies on the retained primary document. See [rule engine](../src/rule_engine/engine.py), [retained Act excerpt](../research/source_ledgers/institutions/page_89.txt), and [probe log](probes_retry.log).

A residual inconsistency matters for comparisons: the rule engine's public default remains `legacy_synthetic`, and Monte Carlo calls that default while the current behavioral path rejects fragmentation. Monte Carlo output does not expose the frequency of synthetic resolutions. Hence a behavioral-versus-Taylor difference can reflect different aggregation rules. Require the same declared resolver in both tracks, count unresolved/synthetic cases, and retain them in results. A categorical action menu is itself an approximation to voting on a proposed resolution; the current code usefully admits this.

**Benchmarks and inference.** The three simple historical baselines are indispensable. I independently recomputed their dissent counts. The all-hold evaluation allows a trivial rate predictor to be perfect, and lag persistence is better than the mock on dissent. The saved zero paired rate-loss differences, zero-width resampling interval and p=1 describe this plateau; they are not evidence of equivalence across other monetary regimes or of a sharply identified model effect. Eight serially related meetings provide little independent support. Seeds, members, rounds and Monte Carlo draws are not additional historical observations.

A serious current defect is that the validation gate uses the union of rate and stance dissent. I constructed a case where the only actual rate dissenter is predicted solely as a stance dissenter: union F1 is 1.0, rate-dissent F1 is zero, and the gate passes. The actual unmasked study likewise passes despite missing all six rate-dissent events. The channel metrics are already computed but not gate requirements. Gate passage also does not require beating any baseline, multiple rate classes or a minimum informative sample. It should be called a descriptive threshold flag until those criteria exist. See [metrics.py](../src/validation/metrics.py), [backtest aggregation](../src/backtest/backtest_runner.py), and [stress_probes.log](stress_probes.log).

The newer bounded Taylor fit is an interpretable predictive comparator, not an identified rational optimum. It fits committee actions with fixed smoothing and constrained coefficients; stance comes from a separate hand-coded rule. My explicitly labeled reviewer diagnostic fitted the reduced-input version on the 17 calibration meetings, using rho=.75, 100 bootstrap draws and seed 17. It produced r-star and alpha effectively at zero, gamma about .6399, and evaluation MAE 12.5 bps versus zero for hold. The fit is reproducible in [reviewer_fitted_taylor.json](reviewer_fitted_taylor.json). This negative diagnostic suggests sensitivity to constraints/regime/input choices; it is not a new project result or an optimization attempt.

The legacy member coefficients are constants, despite a “calibrated” comment in [rational_panel_runner.py](../src/rational/rational_panel_runner.py). Gaussian CPI/gap/Fed noise in Monte Carlo has assumed scales, independence across variables and shared draw inputs across members; those distributions are not estimated uncertainty about beliefs or future policy. Clipping Fed rates and discretizing into a bounded menu can create nonlinear saturation. A zero categorical shock response at +50 bps does not imply zero continuous response or macroeconomic resilience. Report continuous targets and saturation alongside discrete votes, and vary both coefficients and shock/noise assumptions.

For future inferential work, predeclare primary channel-specific endpoints, baseline superiority margins, failure handling and multiplicity families. Use meeting-level paired loss vectors, expanding-window comparisons and enough rate/stance transitions. The implemented block bootstrap and explicit sign-flip assumptions are useful, but a block-size-two bootstrap on eight meetings is fragile and a sign-flip assumption is not established by naming it. Report block-length sensitivity and do not infer broad equivalence from a null. Probabilistic claims require repeat live sampling and calibration/proper scoring; categorical outputs alone cannot establish calibrated probabilities.

**Counterfactuals and attribution.** Current attribution code carefully pairs frozen inputs/settings/seeds and labels effects simulator-local. Outgoing-information removal retains the donor's counted ballot and allows everyone to respond; it is not member removal and not a Shapley decomposition. Casting removal holds ballots fixed and resolves the tie by synthetic status quo; any delta is relative to that chosen tie policy. Macro muting changes values and economic content, rather than merely withholding information. These descriptions should survive into every exported table.

The economic shocks are assumed bundles. A monsoon scenario adds 0.458 times the food change to headline CPI and stipulates activity/gap drags; a Fed scenario independently adds inflation and drains liquidity. No estimated transmission model makes these real-world intervention responses. My zero-dose Fed probe (`fed_hike_bps=0`, `inr_depreciation=0`) changes headline CPI from 5.08 to 5.38. Fixed headline/core/liquidity changes are not functions of the requested Fed shock. This can be a valid named compound scenario, but not a zero-centered Fed dose-response API. Separate component shocks or explicitly parameterize the full bundle, then test zero-dose identity, sign and dose sensitivity. See [scenario_runner.py](../src/scenarios/scenario_runner.py) and [probes_retry.log](probes_retry.log).

No retained current live intervention experiment validates persuasion, institutional power, welfare or policy transmission. Even accurate baseline prediction would not independently identify those causal effects: multiple mechanisms can reproduce the same votes. Identifying external effects would require a structural argument and outside validation beyond prompt interventions. The current local-scope labels are scientifically appropriate.

**Implementation and reproducibility defects beyond the central evidence gap.** The 194 repository tests pass under the corrected offline harness. Additional probes found issues not caught by those tests:

1. `_generate_mock` searches for “Prevailing Policy Stance”, while `to_prompt_text` emits “Prevailing stance”. A supplied accommodative October 2020 packet therefore produces withdrawal of accommodation for Bhide. This affects mock coverage outside the hold-period evaluation and reinforces that mock accuracy is not economic validation.
2. `ReportBuilder.generate_markdown_report` formats nullable precision as a float. Passing the valid masked summary raises `TypeError`. Its report text also incorrectly cites §45ZL for casting votes and unconditionally asserts empirical/institutional achievements, without requiring evidence-type provenance. Fix both null handling and evidence-aware claim generation.
3. Source-to-panel rebuilding is incomplete in this snapshot: `vintage_panel.py` requires an excluded adjudicated-label artifact at a path outside the supplied scientific output set. I checked its absence, without reading or searching for the excluded material. Retained study replay succeeds, but a clean rebuild from raw sources does not have all required scientific dependencies here. Export the needed label input as a standalone scientific ledger.
4. Five Makefile verification/reproduction script targets are absent. Do not interpret this as a claim that the active parent project lacks them; only snapshot-level reproducibility was assessed. Several old entry points still use legacy inputs and stale assertions, and the stage-2 script requests an all-panel run the newer research mode rejects. Provide one documented offline snapshot reproduction command and clearly retire incompatible entry points.
5. The expanded-panel stance vocabulary and roster are not integrated into the current simulation contracts. Resolve those before expanding evaluation; do not silently map an unsupported stance onto an existing class.

The current infrastructure nevertheless has important strengths: strict JSON and schema handling, member-identity checks, immutable scientific inputs, request/schema/seed metadata, distinction between mocks and cache replay, fail-closed research paths, frozen provenance and paired intervention checks. Full four-condition replay verifies much more than a collection of passing unit tests. None of those checks authenticates model-provider receipts independently, guarantees deterministic live outputs or substitutes for substantive validation.

**Relationship to prior work and contribution.** The bundled Kazinnik–Sinclair draft already supplies the broad idea of LLM committee deliberation beside a numerical voting comparator. The [authors' GW working paper](https://www2.gwu.edu/~forcpgm/2025-005.pdf) likewise describes shared initial beliefs and a generalized Bayesian voting track. The present Taylor comparator is a different design; a difference from it cannot automatically be labeled a behavioral bias. The potentially distinctive contribution is RBI-specific, release-aware data and voting/dissent measurement, coupled with auditable experiments. Demonstrating that contribution requires usable data/protocol artifacts and a nontrivial empirical comparison, not only transferring the FOMC architecture.

A public [SSRN record](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5424097) retrieved in search describes a later revision with a much larger backtest, whereas the accessible GW PDF retains the earlier demonstration. I did not reproduce those outside results or resolve version differences. A novelty claim should pin versions and compare methods directly. This was a targeted primary-literature check, not a systematic literature review. Raw tool results and failed fetches are preserved in `web_*.json`.

**Check ledger and limitations.** [pytest_retry.log](pytest_retry.log) records 194 passed in 16.18 seconds. I did not execute paid or live-model simulations, reconstruct all legacy numerical tables, fetch every official source anew, exhaustively reannotate every personal statement, prove completeness of every real-time release set, or audit every possible provider/network failure. Hash/semantic replay is not independent live replication. Four CPI tables and first/last GDP/Fed inputs were directly sampled; all current snapshot hashes/cutoff contracts and all truth resolution-excerpt links were checked. The later coding comparison is complete for the stated compatible fields, not a declaration of universal semantic agreement. Public retrieval of the Act PDFs and October 2026 resolution failed; those limitations are retained. The verdict rests on reproducible current evidence and identified methodological limits, not on any presumed prior assessment.

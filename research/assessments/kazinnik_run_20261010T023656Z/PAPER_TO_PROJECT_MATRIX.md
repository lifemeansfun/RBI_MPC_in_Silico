The Taylor track is explicitly retained as a **mechanical reaction-function comparator**. Implementing the paper's Bayesian numerical model is unnecessary to the selected ballot-visibility question, but its absence prevents a Bayesian replication claim. A Bayesian implementation would also require declared choices for ambiguities in the available draft and missing materials; writing an equation implementation alone would not reproduce the paper's experiments.

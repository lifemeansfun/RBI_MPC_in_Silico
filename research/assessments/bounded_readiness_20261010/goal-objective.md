1\. ESTABLISH THE SCIENTIFIC BOTTLENECKS\
\
Create a concise evidence inventory and assess these mandatory criteria:\
\
A. Contribution:\
A clear, important research question and a defensible contribution relative to verified primary literature. Distinguish established contributions from hypotheses.\
\
B. Data and timing:\
Traceable sources, release/vintage availability, valid information cutoffs, explicit missingness and defensible outcome labels. Exposed historical data must remain labeled retrospective.\
\
C. Experimental identification:\
Comparisons that can answer the declared research question, meaningful simple baselines, comparable information sets and controls that separate persona, deliberation and institutional-rule effects.\
\
D. Empirical evidence:\
Actual model execution where the claim requires it, informative policy regimes and transitions, preserved failures and independently checked results. Mock generations, cache replay and software tests cannot satisfy this criterion.\
\
E. Statistics and measurement:\
Precisely defined endpoints, separate rate/stance/dissent channels, appropriate meeting-level or temporal uncertainty, missing-label sensitivity and no pseudoreplication.\
\
F. Reproducibility:\
Frozen inputs/settings, recorded commands, environment and hashes, raw generations, complete failure records and a working reconstruction/evaluation path.\
\
G. Claim validity:\
Conclusions proportional to evidence. Simulator interventions establish simulator-local effects unless external causal validity is demonstrated.\
\
Mark every criterion PASS, FAIL or UNVERIFIED with concrete evidence references. Passing a numerical threshold or receiving reviewer approval cannot substitute for these criteria.\
\
Identify the smallest set of decisive experiments or source checks needed to resolve the central uncertainties. Prioritize those over additional engineering polish.\

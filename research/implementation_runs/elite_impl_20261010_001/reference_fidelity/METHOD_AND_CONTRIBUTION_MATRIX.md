## What the current implementation adds to the comparison

The implemented engineering contract retains actual R1-R3 outputs for six members and R4 outputs for five non-Governor members: **23 common calls**, followed by fresh Governor draws. H withholds the realized final ballots and final rationales. B exposes only member/rate/stance tuples labelled binding ballots; N exposes identical tuples labelled nonbinding preferences. Every branch retains earlier arguments, earlier preferences and the Governor's own state. H therefore tests the incremental final information, not absence of peer preferences.

Candidate contrasts are B-H for final-information visibility and B-N for binding status versus numerical anchoring. Six raw ballots support rate, stance and joint pair agreement even when institutional aggregation is unresolved. With five peers fixed, only five of 15 unordered pairs can change; the global absolute agreement-effect ceiling is 1/3, with tighter prefix-specific ranges. Failed planned branches remain in the accounting and failure-bound analysis.

This creates an inspectable simulator-local intervention with precise information projection and retained realized outputs. **It does not establish a unique scientific contribution, meaningful effect, forecasting advantage, psychological fidelity or real-policy importance.** These require the revised reference methods, broader targeted literature comparisons, frozen scientific evaluation and human/domain evidence. No live scientific generation was performed for this record.

## Retained evidence and reproduction

`reference_methods.json` contains machine-readable facts, page pointers, inference labels and open gates. `retrieval_records.json` binds public URLs, UTC access times, response status, byte lengths and SHA-256, including failed responses. `extraction_records.json` binds pypdf page extractions; exact PDFs and extracted pages are in `sources/`. Web-reader/indexed records preserve the revision and version evidence separately from successful full-text acquisition. The failed Takano web screenshot fell back to a local render, `takano_table2_page12.png`; the missing Table 2 row is visible in the PDF itself.

Acquisition used `acquire_primary_sources.py`: one attempt per nine declared official URLs, 35-second request timeout, 12 MB response bound, no authentication, retries or bypasses. The retained GWU file was checked before copying. Re-running acquisition requires a new destination because source files are created exclusively. `command_records.json` and `environment.json` retain command/environment details; `file_manifest.json` hashes the finished evidence package. Public documents are preserved for internal reference checking, not redistributed as licensed project assets. Failure, abstract-only and version-mismatch records remain missing evidence rather than being promoted to full-method findings.

PRESS RELEASE
CONSUMER PRICE INDEX NUMBERS ON BASE 2012=100 FOR RURAL,
URBAN AND COMBINED FOR THE MONTH OF JUNE 2024
I. Key highlights:
1. Year-on-year inflation rate based on All India Consumer Price Index (CPI) number is 5.08%
(Provisional) for the month of June, 2024. Corresponding inflation rate for rural and urban is
5.66% and 4.39%, respectively.
2. Inflation for sub-groups viz. ‘Eggs’, ‘Spices’, ‘Meat & fish’ and ‘Pulses & products’ have
decreased as compared to May 2024.

June 2024 over June 2023), based on General Indices and CFPIs are given as follows:

IV. Response rate: The price data are collected from selected 1114 urban Markets and 1181 villages
covering all States/UTs through personal visits by field staff of Field Operations Division of NSO,
MoSPI on a weekly roster. During the month of June 2024, NSO collected prices from 99.7% villages
and 98.6% urban markets while the market-wise prices reported therein were 88.9% for rural and
93.0% for urban.

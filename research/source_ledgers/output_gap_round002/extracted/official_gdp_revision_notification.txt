                                          Notification

It is hereby brought to the notice of all concerned that the release dates of the release of First
Revised Estimates (FRE), Second Revised Estimates (SRE) and Third Revised Estimates
(TRE) of National Income, Consumption Expenditure, Saving and Capital Formation have
been revised and the press note on these estimates will be released on the last working day of
February each year instead of last working day of January. Accordingly, press note on FRE for
FY 2021-22, SRE for FY 2020-21 and TRE for FY 2019-20 is scheduled to be released on 28th
February, 2023.

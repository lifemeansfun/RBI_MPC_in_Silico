                NATIONAL ACCOUNTS STATISTICS - 2025 PUBLICATION

        The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) has released the
‘National Accounts Statistics – 2025’ publication, a comprehensive source of data on the
national income, production, and expenditure aggregates of the Indian economy. The publication
offers more granular view of the national accounts, through structured data statements and serves
as a critical statistical input for policymakers, researchers, analysts, and stakeholders in
understanding and evaluating the performance and structural changes in the Indian economy.

2.     This publication follows the First Revised Estimates of GDP for the FY 2023–24 and Final
Estimates for FY 2022-23, which were released in February 2025, providing headline
macroeconomic indicators.

3.      The publication provides detailed estimates of Gross Domestic Product (GDP), Gross
Value Added (GVA), consumption, savings, capital formation, and related macroeconomic
indicators. These estimates are presented at current and constant (2011-12) prices and are based
on the methodologies in alignment with the United Nations System of National Accounts (SNA).

4.       Key Highlights:

     •   GDP and GVA estimates for the years 2022-23 (Final Estimates) and 2023-24 (First
         Revised Estimates), incorporating updated data inputs from administrative and survey
         sources along with the time series data since the FY 2011-12.
     •   Detailed sectoral analysis of economic performance, covering agriculture, industry, and
         services.
     •   Disaggregated estimates on consumption, savings, and capital formation across
         households, corporate sector, and government.

5.     The ‘National Accounts Statistics – 2025’ statements (Annexure) are available for
download on the MoSPI official website at: https://www.mospi.gov.in/publication/national-
accounts-statistics-2025 .

6.      In addition, the publication, ‘National Accounts Statistics – 2024’ is updated by including
the statements on Sequence of Accounts of different institutional sectors. These statements are
available at: https://www.mospi.gov.in/publication/national-accounts-statistics-2024 .

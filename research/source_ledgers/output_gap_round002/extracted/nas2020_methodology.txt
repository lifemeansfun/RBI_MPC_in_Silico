NOTES ON METHODOLOGY AND REVISIONS IN THE ESTIMATES


       The new series on National Accounts Statistics (NAS) was introduced through a Press Release on
January 30, 2015 with base year 2011-12 in place of previous series with base year 2004-05. Later, a
brochure titled “Changes in Methodology and Data Sources in the new series of National Accounts (base
year 2011-12)” was released on 26th June, 2015 incorporating details of both the database and the
methodology employed in estimation of various aggregates. The estimates presented in this issue of NAS
are based on the methodology given in the above-mentioned brochure.

      The present issue on NAS contains statements on macroeconomic aggregates including sectoral
analysis, accounts for Non-Financial Corporations, Financial Corporations, General Government,
Households and Rest of the World. Besides these, it also includes aggregates by economic activities and
disaggregated statements.


             REVISION OF ESTIMATES DUE TO FRESH/ REVISED DATA

         The first revised estimates and the estimates for the previous years of macro-economic aggregates
and their disaggregated components published in NAS 2019 have undergone revision due to fresh/revised
data becoming available. Sources of data utilized in NAS 2020 for preparing estimates for the years 2016-
17, 2017-18 and 2018-19, which have contributed the most in revision of the estimates, are given below:


A:      Output and Value Added
1    Crops
      1.1    Production estimates - For 2017-18, final estimates of major Crops, namely cereals, pulses,
             oilseeds, sugars and fibers released by the DESAg for all States/UTs and final estimates for
             horticulture crops, namely fruits and vegetables, condiments and spices, released by
             Horticulture Statistics Division, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare for most of the
             States/UTs have been used. For 2018-19, fourth advance estimates of major Crops released by
             the DESAg and final estimates for horticulture crops released by Horticulture Statistics
             Division, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare have been used. For 2016-17, final
             estimates of State specific crops like banana, potato, onion etc., received from DESs of States/
             UTs, have been used.
      1.2    Land use Statistics – For the years 2016-17 and 2017-18, the latest area under crops released
             by the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare for some crops and that received from
             DESs of States/ UTs for remaining crops have been used.
      1.3    Prices data – For 2016-17 and 2017-18, updated prices of agricultural commodities received
             from DESs of States/UTs have been applied. For 2018-19, where price data were not available,
             corresponding WPIs have been applied on prices of 2017-18.

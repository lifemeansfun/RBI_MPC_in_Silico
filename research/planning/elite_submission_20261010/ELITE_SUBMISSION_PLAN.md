# RBI MPC In Silico: plan for a defensible leading-journal submission

**Proposal dated 10 October 2026. This is a research plan, not a registered protocol, completed repair, new experiment, or publication-readiness verdict.**

The objective is a scientifically valid, Kazinnik-anchored RBI adaptation with one consequential contribution that survives appropriate comparisons, uncertainty analysis and independent scrutiny. Technical correctness is necessary; a stronger title, more agents, more repeated generations and favorable AI reviews cannot establish that contribution. Valid null or negative findings may support a valuable paper. Inadequate identification, data or precision cannot.

Implementation, future funding and manuscript work would belong to a separately authorized program with a new protocol and ledger. ## 3. Gate 2 — Reconstruct defensible inputs, labels and personas

**Owners: source/provenance team; two independent human coders; domain adjudicator.**

Build a complete meeting inventory from the earliest source-supported period through the registration cutoff, including scheduled and emergency meetings. Use all eligible meetings in the primary historical cohort. Outcome-stratified development diagnostics are allowed when declared; do not select evaluation meetings because a model succeeds, a transition is convenient, or a following outcome changes. Report exclusions, roster regimes, policy classes, transitions, missing targets and their implications.

- **Information availability:** each observation carries definition/unit, reference period, release date/time or conservative availability day, vintage, original source bytes/hash and transformation. Preserve exclusive premeeting cutoffs. Publication-day-only evidence must precede the cutoff conservatively. Do not use revised modern series as if available historically. Verify CPI/food/core definitions; CFPI and broader food-and-beverages CPI are not interchangeable.
- **Missing macro series:** acquire authentic vintage evidence for each proposed variable. Drop unsupported variables from the frozen common information set or retain explicit missingness under a declared policy. Do not invent values. Full Taylor specifications require defensible real-time output-gap history; any gap-free reduced comparator is named separately. One-sided estimation must use only then-available history, with endpoint and revision sensitivity.
- **Rosters:** source appointments, effective tenures, Governor/Deputy Governor roles and known vacancies before cutoff. Distinguish appointed roster, expected attendance and realized attendance. A forecast may not use postmeeting attendance silently. A conditional historical simulation may use an explicitly oracle roster, but its results cannot close a premeeting predictive-validity gate.
- **Outcome coding:** independently double-code official resolutions and member statements, blind to model predictions. Keep exact preferred rate, rate direction, committee stance, literal resolution ballot, exact-rate disagreement, formal stance dissent, reservations and guidance disagreement as separate nullable channels. Version stance ontology across regimes, including supported labels currently absent. Retain quoted evidence, coder disagreement, adjudication and agreement statistics; never infer a missing formal ballot from a reservation.
- **Personas:** convert expanding dated source histories into executable MemberPersona objects with provenance and frozen extraction rules. Publicly expressed emphasis is measurable; latent policy utility and cognitive diversity are hypotheses. Validate extracted dimensions against held-out human-coded text, simple lexical/topic/scaling alternatives, extraction stability and temporal consistency. Prevent same-meeting statements and evaluation labels from entering a persona.
- **Entry and missing history:** predeclare eligibility for the history-conditioned primary analysis. Run a separately labeled role-only cold-start condition if scientifically needed; do not fabricate a personal history or bypass provenance flags. Report how exclusions remove transitions or change the population. Broader individual-realism claims require evidence that survives this limitation.

Official minutes supply published positions and votes, rather than a complete trace of private initial beliefs and deliberation. They cannot validate a generated round-by-round persuasion process. The [RBI minutes publication example](https://www.rbi.org.in/CommonPerson/english/scripts/PressReleases.aspx?Id=2601) illustrates that distinction.

**Closeout evidence:** a portable source bundle, machine-readable eligibility/exclusion table, per-meeting input and persona manifests, versioned label codebook, independent coding record and complete clean-source reconstruction. Hash consistency is necessary but does not prove original-source authenticity or historical availability.

### Candidate Governor experiment

Generate R1–R3 for six members and R4 for five non-Governor members once per prefix replicate: 23 common calls. Hold macro/personas, Governor own state, earlier arguments, identity/order and those five realized final choices fixed.

Use three final-Governor conditions:

| Condition | Final information supplied | Role |
|---|---|---|
| H | Final ballot tuples withheld; final rationales withheld | Primary comparator |
| B | Only five final member/rate/stance tuples, described as binding votes | Candidate ballot-visibility treatment |
| N | The identical tuples, described as nonbinding preferences | Rival explanation: numerical anchoring versus binding status |

B–H is the candidate primary visibility contrast. B–N is a prespecified mechanism check, with multiplicity or hierarchical testing declared before evaluation. N is an artificial information-status intervention; it is not a claim about actual RBI voting. Final-rationale visibility, authority labels and Governor position belong to separately identified secondary contrasts if Gate 1 makes them necessary. Do not execute a full Cartesian product without a question and power justification.

Audit both byte-level differences and semantic information. Have blinded readers infer final votes from H's earlier arguments. If values are already recoverable, interpret B–H as explicit final commitment disclosure conditional on those arguments, rather than removal of all peer-preference information. Do not redact the entire debate after observing a null to obtain a stronger effect.

For six valid ballots define joint agreement as agreeing unordered member pairs divided by 15. If c(a) is the count of five fixed peers choosing joint rate/stance category a, then:

**agreement(B) − agreement(H) = [c(Governor_B) − c(Governor_H)] / 15.**

The global bound is ±1/3. With five distinct peer categories, the maximum magnitude is 1/15; different prefixes have different attainable ranges. Enumerate these partitions and feasible Governor choices before judging whether a proposed effect margin is attainable. Measure exact-rate agreement, stance agreement, bps dispersion and initial-to-final change separately.

Keep consensus computable when six valid ballots do not yield a unique committee resolution. Implement verified quorum/majority/casting behavior for supported institutional cases; disclose full-roster, proposal/menu and multi-option resolution assumptions. The [retrieved RBI Act consolidation](https://www.dicgc.org.in/sites/default/files/2024-10/RBIA1934170510.pdf) is amended through 2022; check applicable later amendments/Gazette before calling legal rules current. Governor-last prompting and final-ballot visibility are simulator choices, not statutory requirements.

### Comparators and mechanism claims

Shock scenarios require separate identified channels or named compound interventions; omit unsupported real-policy transmission, equilibrium and welfare claims.

## 5. Gate 4 — Determine precision, cost and model robustness before registration

**Owner: statistician/methods lead with execution owner and domain specialist.**

Declare the effect that matters before examining pilot treatment estimates. A one-pair change, 1/15, is an interpretable measurement reference, not automatically an important contribution. Set the practical effect or equivalence margin, committee-decision relevance and forecast-comparison margin from the question and domain rationale. Freeze these before variance-based sample planning.

A bounded development pilot may use at most six source-complete exposed development meetings, two independent prefix replicates per meeting and two fresh Governor draws for each H/B/N branch: at most **348 generation calls**. Select development cases using a recorded rule covering available regimes; exclude these generated outcomes from the confirmatory cohort or mark their reuse exploratory. The pilot estimates latency, billed tokens, formatting failures, generation/prefix variance and integration feasibility. Two prefix replicates do not provide a reliable variance estimate alone; use conservative variance ranges and simulation sensitivity when sizing the main study.

For the primary historical estimand, average branch draws within prefix and prefixes within meeting, then average meetings with preregistered weights. Distinguish Monte Carlo uncertainty for this fixed cohort from uncertainty about future meetings. For temporal generalization use justified regime/block sensitivity; few effective blocks must remain visible. Bootstrap draw count cannot replace meetings. Model families and committee members are not additional independent meetings.

Specify the target interval width and, if hypothesis testing is central, the detection/equivalence design and operating characteristics across plausible effect/variance/dependence ranges. Determine meeting count, prefix count and final-Governor draw count jointly. Increase stochastic replication only while generation noise is the limiting factor. If temporal evidence is limiting, obtain new meetings or restrict claims; additional draws cannot solve it.

Budget using the exact call graph and measured billed input/output, including thinking tokens, provider token-count requests, any grounding, retries and unresolved charges. Current [Google Standard pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing#gemini-3.1-flash-lite) lists USD0.25/million text input and USD1.50/million output including thinking for the configured model. Price must be captured again at freeze. The old 1,150-call illustrative bound is about USD22.37 only under its verified token-bound assumptions. It is not a complete submission-program budget or authenticated available balance.

Reconcile historical provider account evidence and prior spending; never initialize a blank ledger to manufacture remaining allowance. If historical allocation cannot be authenticated, a separately authorized new allocation must explicitly preserve that uncertainty and old counters. No new spending follows from this plan.

Account for RPM, TPM, daily quota, latency and interruption. [Google's limits](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits) are project-wide and apply across all dimensions. At configured 5 RPM, 348 calls have a 69.6-minute pacing floor; the previous 1,150-call design has a 230-minute floor. Daily quota may dominate both. These are lower bounds, not completion-time promises. Hardware measured here is Apple M1 with 8 GiB memory; local inference or new accelerator access is unbenchmarked.

Freeze provider, revision where available, settings, structured-output schema and prompt templates. Counterbalance branch order and interleave conditions within a prefix to reduce service-time confounding. If a reported revision changes, pause the affected condition and record a new epoch; do not pool silently. If the provider cannot identify stable weights, scope results to the service/date epoch and report that reproducibility limitation.

For claims extending beyond one service, predeclare a second independently trained model family and a neutral prompt robustness check before final outcomes; choose them by availability and capability criteria rather than favorable effect. A contradictory replication narrows the claim. Lack of funding leaves model-generalization unverified, not a reason to substitute an undisclosed cheaper model.

**Gate:** a numeric execution manifest specifying approved dollar/call/token/retry/time limits, resource reserve, exact schedule, achievable precision and stopping rules. If the design cannot fit resources or answer the question precisely enough, stop or register a narrower distinct claim before evaluation. Do not reduce rigor after seeing results.

## 6. Gate 5 — Register and run the live study; separate stronger validation

Publish or externally timestamp the executable protocol, source/code/prompt hashes, exposed-data declaration, cohort/exclusions, primary contrast/endpoint, margins, failure denominators and complete analysis before the main live study. Registration may be public or embargoed; local hashes alone are not proof of timing. Keep a signed exposure log and segregate new evaluation outputs from development owners where feasible.

Every attempt has immutable experiment/condition/meeting/prefix/branch-draw identity, actual request bytes, raw response, parsed result, provider metadata/usage, timestamps, costs and status. Cache replay remains replay; live stochastic conditions require fresh calls. Use one shared accountant and one scheduler for all owners.

Predeclare transport retry rules; retain the original attempt and charge uncertainty. Never retry a valid but inconvenient answer, repair malformed scientific answers asymmetrically, replace missing branches, or choose the best generation. Scientific schema failures remain failures under the planned schedule; provenance/accounting/cancellation failures halt the run. Preserve pending pairs on stop. Resume only the same frozen specification with unchanged identities and declared rules.

Analyze planned denominators. Report failures by condition and time/regime. Complete-pair estimates are sensitivity analyses when failures are treatment-dependent; add prespecified sharp worst-case bounds using the attainable endpoint ranges. Missing labels, absent stances and unresolved institutional decisions remain separate missingness channels. Zero positives and sparse dissent require transparent denominators and uncertainty.

### Prospective prediction

If predictive usefulness remains a claim, timestamp every model and baseline prediction before future meeting outcomes, using a fixed public information cutoff. Keep later member votes/stances sealed until their official release. Fit only on previously available evidence under a frozen update algorithm; model/prompt changes create a new protocol epoch.

Report exact-rate accuracy, bps MAE, direction and stance separately; include class counts, confusion matrices, paired baseline differences and uncertainty. Probability claims require calibrated probabilities and proper scores. Historical hindsight and pretraining contamination remain qualifications; masking identity/date does not establish a clean historical holdout.

Set a calendar or meeting-count stopping rule and precision plan before starting. More calendar time does not guarantee cuts, hikes or dissent. A year of predominantly hold decisions may remain inconclusive. Do not promise that one future meeting or an arbitrary number of repeats establishes forecasting skill.

### Human or institutional validation

For claims about persuasion or decision processes, add a separately registered, ethically reviewed experiment with qualified participants and independently coded outcomes. Randomize vote-information status/authority at the committee level; record private initial choices and confidence before interaction and final choices afterward. Use grouped analysis for repeated scenarios, a priori precision/power, blinded scoring and explicit participant expertise.

A controlled decision task may have a known loss function or true state; that supports decision quality in that task. Matching an observed RBI decision measures prediction, not policy optimality. Student/expert-laboratory results do not automatically establish effects in the actual RBI MPC. Public minutes cannot fill the missing human pre/post process data. If this validation cannot be obtained, constrain the paper to simulator effects and predictive performance actually measured.

## 8. Gate 7 — Write and assess the submission against the evidence

Manuscript creation is a later stage of this submission plan; no existing manuscript is edited by the present planning task.

Write the central contribution after the evidence gates, with assumptions and claim scope fixed by the results. Every abstract claim, table cell and figure has an artifact link. Report primary estimates and intervals, practical significance, condition-specific failures, nulls, contrary replications and exploratory departures. Remove unsupported claims of rationality, cognitive diversity, equilibrium, welfare, human realism or untouched historical prediction.

Provide reproducible code/data or lawful source acquisition scripts, machine-readable results, all protocol versions, codebook, exposure log and complete model/prompt/resource disclosures. Record source licensing and human-participant consent/privacy where applicable. Follow the selected journal's current data/code and AI-disclosure policy at submission time.

Choose the target by what the completed paper establishes: computational/AI methods for generalizable simulation-method evidence, forecasting for credible predictive evidence, or monetary/behavioral economics for externally supported economic mechanisms. Inspect closely comparable recent papers and obtain a domain specialist's fit assessment. Journal prestige cannot repair an inadequate design.

### Submission gates

- No unresolved critical source timing, treatment identification, measurement or provenance flaw.
- One important contribution justified against exact verified prior work, with limitations where revised methods remain unavailable.
- Adequate precision for the stated inference; a well-designed informative null is acceptable. Imprecision is not equivalence.
- Claims generalize only across the meetings, models, prompts, humans and institutions actually supported.
- Central results independently recomputed; portable reproduction verified.
- Manuscript, supplementary material and release bundle describe the same frozen scientific version.
- Outstanding critical criticism resolved with evidence, or the corresponding claim withdrawn.

A gate failure produces an explicit incomplete/unsupported outcome and retained artifacts. Journal acceptance and two favorable AI opinions are not completion tests.

## 9. Sequence, estimates and unresolved dependencies

Execute dependencies in this order: contribution/reference audit → source/label/construct audit → identified runner and independent oracles → funded development pilot/precision design → executable registration → live study and prospectively sealed collection → independent recomputation/external validation → manuscript and submission.

Source collection and literature work can proceed in parallel. Source/persona, branching and independent-oracle implementation can proceed in parallel after interfaces freeze. Main-study result access is separated from ongoing development. Do not run competing jobs when measuring execution performance.

Planning estimate under available human collaborators: allow roughly 8–12 weeks for literature/source foundations, integration, pilot and a retrospective live study, followed by independent analysis and writing. This is not a deadline guarantee; data availability, coding burden and budget may extend it. Prospective prediction operates over future official meetings and may require multiple years for regime variation and useful precision. A human mechanism study has its own recruitment/ethics/sample schedule. The earlier eight-hour allowance is not a plausible calendar for this program.

Unresolved dependencies are actual future funding, authenticated quota/accounting, accessible revised methods, a source-audited eligible cohort, qualified human/domain collaborators and attainable precision. Final sample sizes, model-replication costs and an exact journal cannot honestly be fixed before those observations. The gates above specify how to decide them before evaluation rather than inventing reassuring numbers now.
